## Unverified
- Real PEFT key prefix for `Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration` (assumed `base_model.model.model.language_model.layers.N...`; converter regexes on `layers.N.` and skips vision/audio keys).
- Adapter trained on bf16 base applied to 4-bit base (quantisation mismatch) - quality must be measured.
- Gemma 4 E4B nuance: PEFT on `google/gemma-4-E4B-it` may wrap clipped/audio/vision linears; restrict `target_modules` to a regex on `language_model.layers`.
- PEFT adapter in this venv: `peft` is not installed (no test against a real PEFT file).

## Training time extrapolation
Smoke: 20 iters in ~17.6 s wall incl. load (~10 s load, so roughly 0.4 s/iter on ~120-token examples). 1100 ex x 3 epochs = 3300 iters at bs1 -> ~22 min at that speed; with real tickets (~300+ tokens) assume 2-3x -> 45-70 min. Peak mem should stay ~6-8 GB.
